# Simulating a complex cardiac calcium model

We have discussed the biology of EC coupling and the mathematical equations governing the individual pumps and exchangers. Having already built a simple calcium cycling model in Exercise 19.1, it is now time to tackle a full-scale cardiac model.

In this chapter, you will face the **Jafri-Winslow-Borg (1998)** human cardiomyocyte model — a massive, 31-state system where you will wire the complex Luo-Rudy NCX and RyR Markov models directly into the differential equations.
