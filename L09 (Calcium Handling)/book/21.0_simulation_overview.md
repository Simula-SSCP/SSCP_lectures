# Simulating calcium dynamics

We have discussed the biology of EC coupling and the mathematical equations governing the individual pumps. Now, it is time to bring it all together into a working computational model.

In this chapter, you will complete two Python exercises. First, you will implement a highly simplified, 2-variable ODE model of calcium cycling (based on a sympathetic neuron) to see how Calcium-Induced Calcium Release (CICR) can generate spontaneous oscillations. Second, you will face the daunting **Jafri-Winslow-Borg (1998)** human cardiomyocyte model—a massive, 31-state system where you will wire the complex Luo-Rudy NCX and RyR Markov models directly into the differential equations.
