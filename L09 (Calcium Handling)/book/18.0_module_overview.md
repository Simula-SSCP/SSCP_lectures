# Intracellular calcium handling

In the previous modules, we focused entirely on the outer boundary of the cell. We studied the action potential, the voltage-gated ion channels that generate it, and the equations governing electrical conduction.

In this module, we shift our focus inward. We will explore what happens _inside_ the cytosol following an action potential, focusing primarily on intracellular **calcium handling**.

Calcium is the crucial physiological messenger that couples the electrical activation of the cell to its physical contraction—a beautifully orchestrated process known as **excitation-contraction (EC) coupling**. We will map out the internal structures of the cell, track the exact path of calcium during a heartbeat, and study the mathematical models used to represent the complex pumps and exchangers (like SERCA and NCX) that maintain cellular life. Finally, you will write Python code to simulate these complex calcium cycling dynamics in both simplified neurons and highly detailed human cardiomyocytes.
