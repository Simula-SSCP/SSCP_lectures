# Deriving the cable equation

Before we can simulate traveling waves of electricity, we must mathematically define how current flows through biological cables.

In this chapter, we will trace the history of transmission line theory from Lord Kelvin's early transatlantic telegraph models to Oliver Heaviside's wave equations. We will then apply these exact same principles to the anatomy of a neuron.

By utilizing Ohm's law and the principle of current conservation, we will derive the biological **cable equation**—a partial differential equation (PDE) that dictates how voltage changes over both time and physical space. Finally, we will simplify this equation using dimensional scaling, making it universally applicable across different types of cells.
