# Nonlinear biomechanics

```{note}
The theoretical material for this chapter is currently only available as a slide presentation.
```

This lecture introduces the mathematical framework required to model complex 3D tissue deformation. It shifts from ordinary differential equations to continuum mechanics, defining finite strain tensors, the Cauchy stress tensor, and the hyperelastic constitutive laws required to simulate the beating heart wall.
