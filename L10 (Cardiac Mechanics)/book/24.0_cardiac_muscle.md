# Cardiac muscle

```{note}
The theoretical material for this chapter is currently only available as a slide presentation.
```

This lecture dives into the physiological basis of the Frank-Starling mechanism. It explores the micro-anatomy of the sarcomere, the molecular motors driving the sliding filament theory (myosin and actin), and the profound effect that sarcomere length has on force production and calcium sensitivity.
