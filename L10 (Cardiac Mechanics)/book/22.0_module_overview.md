# Introduction to cardiac mechanics

Up to this point in the course, we have focused primarily on the cell membrane (electrical excitation) and the cytosol (calcium handling). However, the ultimate purpose of this electrophysiological cascade is to generate physical force.

In this module, we zoom out to look at the macroscopic mechanical function of the heart, the 3D physics of tissue deformation, and the systems-level networks that tie everything together.

The theoretical material for this module is currently only available as slide presentations. The chapters in this module will cover:

1. **Ventricular function:** Hemodynamics, stroke work, and the cardiac cycle.
2. **Cardiac muscle:** The sliding filament theory and length-dependent activation.
3. **Nonlinear biomechanics:** Stress, strain, and hyperelastic tissue modeling.
4. **Statistical shape atlases:** Medical imaging, PCA, and morphological data science.
5. **Network modelling:** Boolean logic and the systems biology of biochemical pathways.
