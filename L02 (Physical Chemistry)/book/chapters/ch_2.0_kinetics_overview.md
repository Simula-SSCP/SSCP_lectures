# Fundamentals of chemical kinetics

Cells are highly dynamic environments. To understand how a cell functions, we have to look at how its molecular components interact, bind, and transform over time. Whether we are modeling an ion channel snapping open in the cell membrane or an enzyme processing a substrate, we need a mathematical framework to describe how fast these reactions happen.

This branch of physical chemistry is known as **chemical kinetics**.

In this chapter, we will establish the fundamental rules governing the rates of chemical reactions. We will start with the **Law of Mass Action**, which allows us to link the speed of a reaction directly to the concentration of the molecules involved.

From there, we will:

- Translate chemical equations into systems of Ordinary Differential Equations (ODEs).
- Learn how to computationally solve these ODEs using Python and SciPy.
- Explore reversible reactions and see how they naturally settle into a state of **chemical equilibrium**.

By the end of this chapter, you will have the mathematical and computational tools necessary to model simple, time-dependent molecular interactions from scratch.
