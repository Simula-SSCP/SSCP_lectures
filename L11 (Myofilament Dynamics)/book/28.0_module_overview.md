# Introduction to myofilament dynamics

In the previous modules, we traced the electrical action potential along the cell membrane and watched as it triggered a massive flood of calcium into the cytosol. But how exactly does this chemical cloud of calcium generate physical, mechanical force?

In this module, we dive into the microscopic machinery of the muscle cell: the **myofilaments**.

We will trace the chronological history of computational muscle mechanics. We will start with A.F. Huxley's groundbreaking 1957 model, which first established the mathematics of the sliding filament theory. We will then explore the Razumova (2000) model to understand the complex "cooperativity" that occurs when neighboring proteins interact. Finally, we will arrive at the modern Rice (2008) model, which successfully couples the binding of calcium directly to crossbridge distortion to simulate a realistic, force-generating muscle twitch.
