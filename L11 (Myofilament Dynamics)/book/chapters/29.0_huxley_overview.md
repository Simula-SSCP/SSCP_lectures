# The Huxley (1957) model

The modern era of muscle biomechanics began with A.F. Huxley's 1957 paper, _"Muscle Structure and Theories of Contraction."_ In this chapter, we will walk through the fundamental mathematical assumptions of the sliding filament theory. We will derive the steady-state equations for crossbridge attachment, and explore how the probability of a crossbridge binding to an actin filament depends heavily on its physical displacement. Finally, you will implement this classic model in Python to generate the famous macroscopic Force-Velocity relationship of contracting muscle.
