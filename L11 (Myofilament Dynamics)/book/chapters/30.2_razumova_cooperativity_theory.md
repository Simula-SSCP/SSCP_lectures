# Cooperativity in the Razumova model

The simple four-state model presented in {ref}`the previous section <sec_razumova_basic>` uses constant kinetic rates. While it successfully captures the basic dynamics of crossbridge cycling, it **cannot reproduce** the steep, sigmoidal force-pCa curves observed experimentally. The missing ingredient is **cooperativity**.

The true power of the Razumova model lies in how it handles cooperativity: the transition rates between states ($k_{\mathrm{on}}$, $f$, etc.) are _not_ constant. They dynamically change depending on what the neighbouring proteins are doing!

## Calcium-dependent base rates

Before introducing cooperativity, we first make the activation and deactivation rates depend on calcium concentration:

$$
k_{\mathrm{on}}^u = k_{\mathrm{on}}^0 + \left[k_{\mathrm{on}}^{\mathrm{Ca}} - k_{\mathrm{on}}^0\right] \frac{[\mathrm{Ca}^{2+}]}{\mathrm{Ca}_{50} + [\mathrm{Ca}^{2+}]}
$$

$$
k_{\mathrm{off}}^u = k_{\mathrm{off}}^0 + \left[k_{\mathrm{off}}^{\mathrm{Ca}} - k_{\mathrm{off}}^0\right] \frac{[\mathrm{Ca}^{2+}]}{\mathrm{Ca}_{50} + [\mathrm{Ca}^{2+}]}
$$

where $\mathrm{Ca}_{50} = k^-/k^+$ is the calcium concentration of half-saturation of the thin filament binding sites. The superscript $u$ denotes that these are the base rates *before* cooperativity is applied.

## State subpopulations

To express the cooperative formulas compactly, it is useful to define the following subpopulations as fractions of the total $R_T$:

| Symbol | Definition | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| $\lambda^{\mathrm{off}}$ | $R_{\mathrm{off}} / R_T$ | Fraction of inactive sites |
| $\lambda^{\mathrm{on}}$ | $(D + A_1 + A_2) / R_T$ | Fraction of active sites |
| $\lambda^{A_2}$ | $A_2 / R_T$ | Fraction in the force-generating state |

Note that $\lambda^{\mathrm{off}} + \lambda^{\mathrm{on}} = 1$ by mass conservation.

## The three types of cooperativity

The authors defined three distinct types of cooperative coupling.

:::{admonition} Full Boltzmann derivation
:class: seealso

The detailed derivation of the cooperativity parameters from nearest-neighbour activation energies and Boltzmann statistics is presented in {ref}`sec_razumova_cooperativity`.
:::

### 1. RU-RU Cooperativity (Parameter $u$)

If a regulatory unit is adjacent to another RU that is already turned _on_, it requires less activation energy to turn on itself. Considering all possible nearest-neighbour configurations and assuming a random distribution:

$$
k_{\mathrm{on}}^w = k_{\mathrm{on}}^u \left[1 + \lambda^{\mathrm{on}}(u - 1)\right]^2
$$

$$
k_{\mathrm{off}}^w = k_{\mathrm{off}}^u \left[u - \lambda^{\mathrm{on}}(u - 1)\right]^2
$$

When $u = 1$, there is no cooperativity. When $u > 1$, the activation of a few sites triggers an avalanche of activation in their neighbours.

### 2. XB-XB Cooperativity (Parameter $v$)

If a crossbridge attaches and undergoes a powerstroke ($A_2$), it physically deforms the filament, making it easier for neighbouring detached crossbridges ($D$) to attach and enter the $A_1$ state:

$$
f = f_0 \left[1 + \lambda^{A_2}(e^{v-1} - 1)\right]^2
$$

$$
f' = f_0' \left[1 + \lambda^{A_2}(e^{-(v-1)} - 1)\right]^2
$$

A larger value of $v$ means tighter XB-XB coupling: as more crossbridges reach the force-generating state, the attachment rate $f$ increases while the reverse rate $f'$ decreases.

### 3. XB-RU Cooperativity (Parameter $w$)

Finally, crossbridges and regulatory units interact with each other. If a crossbridge is strongly bound ($A_2$), it physically wedges the regulatory unit open, preventing it from turning off:

$$
k_{\mathrm{on}} = k_{\mathrm{on}}^w \left[1 + \lambda^{A_2}(e^{w-1} - 1)\right]^2
$$

$$
k_{\mathrm{off}} = k_{\mathrm{off}}^w \left[1 + \lambda^{A_2}(e^{-(w-1)} - 1)\right]^2
$$

## Effects of cooperativity on the Force-pCa curve

By tuning the three cooperative parameters ($u$, $v$, and $w$), the Razumova model can perfectly reproduce the steep, sigmoidal force-pCa curves observed in real heart muscle tissue. The figures below illustrate the distinct influence of each parameter:
