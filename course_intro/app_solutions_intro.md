# How to use the solutions

This section contains the solutions to all the exercises provided throughout the course. They are grouped by topic and broken down step-by-step to help you check your work or get unstuck if you hit a wall.

However, we strongly encourage you to treat these solutions as a **last resort**.

Programming and mathematical modeling are not spectator sports. The true value of this summer school lies in the struggle of debugging a broken solver, tracking down a missing minus sign in your ODEs, and finally getting that plot to render correctly. If you jump straight to the solution the moment you feel stuck, you are robbing yourself of that learning process.

**Our recommendation:**

1. Try to solve the exercise completely on your own first.
2. If you get stuck, consult the lecture notes, the Python documentation, or discuss it with your peers.
3. If you are still stuck after a genuine attempt, look _only_ at the specific sub-exercise you need help with, understand the logic, and then return to your own notebook to continue.
