## Welcome to the Simula Summer School in Computational Physiology!

This book contains the lecture material for the Simula Summer School in Computational Physiology (SSCP). It covers the fundamental principles of mathematical modeling in electrophysiology and cardiac muscle mechanics---from molecular-scale biophysics to tissue-level simulations.

The material is designed for graduate students and covers:

- **Physical chemistry and electrochemistry** – reaction kinetics, thermodynamics, and diffusion
- **Membrane biophysics** – ion channels, the Nernst equation, and membrane models
- **Ion channel modeling** – from two-state channels to Markov models
- **Action potential simulation** – building cell models from channel components
- **Electrical conduction** – the cable equation and cardiac tissue modeling
- **Calcium handling** – excitation-contraction coupling and calcium dynamics
- **Cardiac mechanics** – myofilament dynamics and continuum mechanics
- **Computational methods** – the finite element method, FEniCS, and EMI modeling

Each chapter includes theory and code-based exercises that can be launched interactively.
