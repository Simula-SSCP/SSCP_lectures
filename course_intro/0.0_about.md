# About the summer school

Since 2014, Simula Research Laboratory, the University of Oslo, and the University of California, San Diego have organized an annual summer school in computational physiology. The school brings together graduate students to learn the principles underlying mathematical models commonly used in studying the heart and the brain.

Students spend two weeks of formal lectures and project work in June at Simula in Oslo, Norway, followed by 1.5 weeks of workshops, joint project work, and a final presentation in August at the University of California, San Diego (La Jolla, USA). The course is worth 10 ECTS from the University of Oslo.

The summer school is tailored to first- and second-year Ph.D. students and MSc students from both host universities and third-party institutions.
